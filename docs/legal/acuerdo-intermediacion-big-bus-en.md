## Clause 1. Purpose

Party B authorises Party A to market, in Mexico and on its website, the hop-on hop-off sightseeing bus tickets and combined products that Party B offers to resellers on its partner platform, as listed in Annex 1.

1.2. Expandable portfolio: Party A may add new Party B products through an addendum signed by both parties, which updates Annex 1. Until it is signed, the new product is not published.

1.3. Supplements, complementary services and flights: Party B does not sell flights. Tickets are sold as stand-alone sightseeing transport and as a complement to tours in the same city. If a product includes flights in the future, the following applies: flights are issued by the airline and subject to its fares. Travel Puzzle and Big Bus Tours are not liable for schedule changes, cancellations, overbooking or delays attributable to the airline. The customer must report any incident to the airline; Travel Puzzle will provide reasonable assistance to handle it, without guaranteeing results. This clause will be reviewed by legal counsel before signature, in accordance with the Mexican Federal Consumer Protection Law.

1.4. Stand-alone experiences: transfers, day tours, activities and additional services of Party B are added following the procedure in 1.2, under the same rate, cancellation and liability terms of this agreement.

## Clause 2. Role of each party

2.1. Party A acts as a **commercial intermediary**: it promotes, receives requests and charges the end customer the sale price.

2.2. Party B is the **operator** of the services at destination and is responsible for performing the services that Party A books.

2.3. Party A does not operate or guarantee Party B's services. If the failure is Party B's, the claim is submitted to Party B, with a copy to Party A.

3.2. The net rate on the platform is the cost that Party A pays to Party B. [BIG BUS: confirm whether the 20% is calculated on the in-city price or on the discounted online price, and who absorbs local VAT.]

3.4. Only products available to resellers on the platform are sold. Anything not shown on the platform is exclusive to Party B's website and is not resold (Rositsa Grigorova, 11 Nov 2026).

3.5. Tickets are issued on the platform upon payment; Party A gives the customer Party B's voucher.

If payment by bank transfer is used in the future, Party B provides the account details in writing, on company letterhead signed by its representative; any change of account is confirmed by phone or video call before the next payment. 7.1. Each party processes customer data in accordance with the Mexican Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties (LFPDPPP) and its privacy notices.

## Clause 8. Term and termination

8.1. Indefinite term. Each rate season is added to Annex 1 by addendum signed by both parties. The rates of a season apply to trips confirmed within its validity. The expiry of a rate sheet does not terminate the agreement.

8.1.1. Until a new rate sheet is signed, the current rates continue to apply to trips already confirmed.

8.2. Rate updates by email. When Party B changes rates, departures, supplements or cancellation policies of a product in Annex 1, it will notify Party A by email. Party A will confirm by email that it has received the update. Only with that confirmation does Party A update Annex 1, and the rate applies from the date indicated by Party B. This update does not require a new signature of the agreement. The email confirmations form part of the agreement file and are kept.

8.3. Either party may terminate the agreement in writing, with the notice defined by Party A. Trips confirmed before termination are honoured until departure.

## Clause 9. Governing law, disputes and language

9.1. Governing law: the laws of the United Mexican States, including the Commercial Code.

9.2. Disputes: any dispute is resolved by institutional arbitration under the Arbitration Rules of the International Chamber of Commerce (ICC). Seat: Mexico City. Language of the arbitration: Spanish. Number of arbitrators: one, unless the amount exceeds what the rules provide, in which case there will be three. The award is final and binding on the parties.

9.3. Before starting arbitration, the parties will try to resolve the dispute through direct negotiation for 30 calendar days from written notice.

9.4. Language of the agreement: it is signed in Spanish, the language in which it is registered and enforced in Mexico. Any translation is for reference only; in case of doubt, the Spanish text prevails. Party B receives a courtesy English translation, for reference only.

9.5. Enforcement: the arbitral award may be enforced in Mexico and in the United Kingdom under the 1958 New York Convention. [COUNSEL: confirm accession and reservations of the United Kingdom.]

## Clause 10. Force majeure

10.1. Neither party is liable for non-performance caused by force majeure or acts of God, such as natural disasters, pandemics, airport closures, wars or decisions of authorities. The affected party notifies the other in writing within a reasonable time.

10.2. Services not provided due to force majeure are refunded to Party A only if Party B charged for them and did not provide them; the refund to the customer follows Clause 4.
